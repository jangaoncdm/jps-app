# JPS share kit

The QR is the distribution channel. Farmers scan it and tap **Install** once — they never type or
receive a URL. Everything below is built around that.

## The QR

- Poster (A4, print-ready): open `poster.html` from the pilot site and press Ctrl+P.
- QR image on its own (to forward or drop into a notice): `qr-install.png` on the same site.

Both point at the installer page, which detects the phone and does the right thing:

| Phone / app | What the farmer sees after scanning |
|---|---|
| Chrome on Android | One big **ఇన్‌స్టాల్ చేయండి** button → installed |
| Scanned inside WhatsApp | One button that opens Chrome on the same page → then Install |
| iPhone (Safari) | Share → Add to Home Screen, in Telugu |
| Already installed | Opens the app straight away |

Put the poster at: milk pooling points, PVCs/area hospitals, mandal offices, Rythu Vedika meetings.

## WhatsApp forward

Forward the **QR poster image**, with this caption:

```
🐄 జనగామ పశు సేవ — మీ పశువులకు ప్రభుత్వ డాక్టర్ సహాయం, ఉచితం!

జబ్బు వివరాలు + ఫోటో పంపండి → డాక్టర్ మీకు కాల్ చేస్తారు → అవసరమైతే వచ్చి చూస్తారు.
మందుల చీటీ కూడా యాప్‌లోనే.

📲 పై QR ను స్కాన్ చేసి "ఇన్‌స్టాల్" ఒక్కసారి నొక్కండి.

🚑 అత్యవసరమైతే నేరుగా 1962 కి కాల్ చేయండి.
— పశుసంవర్ధక శాఖ, జనగామ జిల్లా
```

A phone cannot scan a QR that is on its own screen, so the person receiving the forward taps the
poster image and scans it from a second phone — or, if they are alone, staff install it for them
during the visit (see below). This is the one gap the QR cannot close on its own.

## Install-per-village process
1. Every vet + Gopalamitra/Pashumitra installs the app themselves first (needed for attendance anyway).
2. Each paramedic installs it for the milk-society secretary and 2–3 lead farmers per GP they cover —
   they scan the paramedic's printed poster, so no link changes hands.
3. Those lead farmers become the village installers; the poster QR covers walk-ins.
4. Watch adoption on the admin dashboard (requests by mandal, last-30-days card).
